## 10 Used Items Marketplace

## Overview

Develop an online marketplace where users can buy and sell second-hand items.

## Core Features

* User accounts
* Item listings
* Image uploads
* Search
* Categories
* Filters
* Messaging
* Reviews
* Listing management

## Possible Extensions

* Price estimation
* Fraud detection
* Personalized recommendations
* Image-based item categorization
* Location-based search
