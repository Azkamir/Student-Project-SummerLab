# 03 Restaurant Ordering System

## Overview

Develop a digital restaurant ordering system that connects customers, staff, kitchen operations, and billing.

## Core Features

* Digital menu
* Food categories
* Customer ordering
* Order tracking
* Kitchen order management
* Table management
* Billing
* Order history

## Possible Extensions

* Online payment
* Food recommendations
* Estimated preparation time
* Customer feedback analysis
* Sales analytics

## Suggested Difficulty

 Intermediate
