## 19 Local Deals Finder

## Overview

Develop a platform that helps users discover nearby discounts, promotions, restaurants, shops, and services.

## Core Features

* Location-based search
* Deals and promotions
* Categories
* Search and filtering
* Business profiles
* Favorites
* Reviews

## Possible Extensions

* Personalized deal recommendations
* User behavior analysis
* Deal ranking
* Location-based notifications
* AI recommendation system
