11 Student Lost & Found

## Overview

Develop a platform that helps students report, search for, and recover lost items.

## Core Features

* Lost item reporting
* Found item reporting
* Image upload
* Search
* Categories
* Location information
* Contact system
* Item status tracking

## Possible Extensions

* Image similarity matching
* Automatic item categorization
* Location-based matching
* Notification system
* AI-assisted matching of lost and found reports

## Suggested Difficulty

 Beginner /  Intermediate
